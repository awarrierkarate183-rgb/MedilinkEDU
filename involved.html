Sponsorship & Fundraising

MediLink is student-led. Individual gifts and company gifts both go to chapters, competitions, and outreach. You should know where the money goes before you give.

🏫

Chapter Growth

Startup resources, advisor support, and student leadership training for new and existing chapters.

🏆

Competition & Learning

Event materials, research tools, and student awards across four annual competitions and the biennial Apex.

🌍

Community Outreach

Service drives and local health-economics initiatives led by student chapters.

📡

Operations & Reach

Website, communications, and network expansion so MediLink can grow without burning out two founders.

Community Ally and Health Advocate sponsors get the perk by moving to Impact Partner or Visionary Sponsor.
