Work with MediLink

This page is for sponsors, partners, and volunteers. Students starting a school should use Start a Chapter, not this page. Open a file for how to give, who attends events, how partners sit in the three lenses, and how to volunteer.

Money goes to starter kits, event materials, service drives, and keeping a two-founder network online. Individual gifts and company gifts fund the same work. They are not the same product. Families, alumni, and students use the individual page. Companies choose Community Ally, Health Advocate, Impact Partner, or Visionary Sponsor.

This site does not list individual donor dollar amounts. Named company levels and official benefits load from the board list on the corporate page. Amounts and extras can be customized with leadership. A gift does not buy a student placement. Shadowing still runs through partners and advisors.

How to choose a path

  • Giving as a person: no individual tier list, no attend perk, same four buckets of work.
  • Giving as a company: four named levels. Impact Partner and Visionary Sponsor attend State and National in person.
  • Want to sit in clinic, finance, or tech with students: that is Partner, not only a check.
  • Want to mentor or judge: that is Volunteer.
Sponsor as an individual See corporate tiers

Impact Partner and Visionary Sponsor can attend and be recognized in person at State and National events. In an Apex year that includes the biennial sponsor networking fair. Community Ally and Health Advocate get the perk by moving up. Individual gifts do not unlock this perk.

Dates come from state boards. This page is not a calendar. Naming a track or award does not change the method judges use. Attendance is recognition and a seat in the room. It is not a student placement and not a vote on cases.

Who attends

Impact Partner and Visionary Sponsor. Official benefits load from data/sponsor-tiers.json on the corporate page.

Who does not, yet

Community Ally, Health Advocate, and individual donors. Move up a named company level, or keep giving without buying a seat.

Hospitals, insurers, and health-tech firms sit in the same three-lens framework students use. Shadowing, internships, judges, and workshops. Leadership sets up each relationship before students are placed. This is not a public job board and does not post legal requirements.

A useful workshop is mapped to a syllabus module. Care-lens judges, Cost-lens judges, and Code-lens judges are a strong fit for Nationals regional rounds. Schools and community groups can help a chapter form and host a local case challenge. Open Partner for the four files.

Partnership info

Mentor, judge, or help a chapter run. Tell us your field so we can match you to a chapter, a panel, or a workshop. A two-hour workshop mapped to a syllabus module is more useful than a generic pep talk. Most chapters need one reliable adult besides the school advisor.

Volunteers back the five student offices. They do not replace them. Matching runs through leadership. Do not expect a public directory of student emails. If the apply button alerts you, the Google Form URL is not in data/forms.config.js yet. Email medi.link.edu@gmail.com until it is.
