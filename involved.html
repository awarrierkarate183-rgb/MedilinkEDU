Work with MediLink

This page is for sponsors, partners, and volunteers. A company should leave knowing how to give, which named tier attends State and National events, and how to offer shadowing. A volunteer should leave knowing how to sit on a panel or mentor a chapter. Students starting a school should use Start a Chapter, not this page. Do not treat this as a job board. Leadership sets up each relationship before students are placed.

01 / Give

How to sponsor

Individual gifts and company gifts both go to chapters, competitions, and outreach. You should know where the money goes before you give: starter kits, event materials, service drives, and the work of keeping a two-founder network online. This site does not list individual donor dollar amounts. Named company levels live on the corporate page.

Give as a family, alum, or student on the individual page. Companies choose a named tier on the corporate page: Community Ally, Health Advocate, Impact Partner, and Visionary Sponsor. There is no separate individual tier list. Amounts and extras on the corporate side can be customized with leadership.

Where it goes

Chapter growth, competition materials, community outreach, and operations. A gift does not purchase a student placement. Shadowing still runs through partners and advisors.

How to give

Individuals email from the individual page. Companies use the corporate tiers and the inquire button. Students launching a school use Start a Chapter, not a sponsor form.

Sponsor as an individual See corporate tiers

Perk

Attend National and State competitions

This perk is available only at Impact Partner and above. Impact Partner and Visionary Sponsor can attend and be recognized in person, including the Apex networking fair in an Apex year. Community Ally and Health Advocate sponsors get the perk by moving to Impact Partner or Visionary Sponsor. Naming a track or award is separate from this attend perk. Ask leadership if you want both.

Who attends

Impact Partner and Visionary Sponsor. In an Apex year that includes the biennial sponsor networking fair. Dates come from state boards. This page is not a calendar.

How to get it

Choose Impact Partner or Visionary Sponsor on the corporate page, or move up from Community Ally or Health Advocate. Individual gifts do not unlock this perk.

02 / Partner

Hospitals, insurers, and health-tech firms

Clinical, finance, and tech partners can offer shadowing, internships, judges, and workshops. They sit in the same three-lens framework students use. Leadership sets up each relationship before students are placed. We do not post legal requirements on this site. Shadowing is not a public job board.

A useful workshop is mapped to a syllabus module. A useful judge panel covers clinic, money, and tech, the same way Nationals is judged. School and community groups can join that pipeline too. Full paths live on the Partner page.

03 / Volunteer

Mentor, judge, or help a chapter run

Tell us your field so we can match you. A two-hour workshop mapped to a syllabus module is more useful than a generic pep talk. Most chapters need one reliable adult besides the school advisor. Roles include academic mentor, competition advisor, outreach coordinator, guest speaker, and community liaison.

Volunteers do not replace chapter officers. They back the five offices that already exist: President, VP of Operations, Finance / Treasury Lead, Technology Lead, and Outreach and Service Lead. Apply on the volunteer page. The form link is set in data/forms.config.js when it is ready.

04 / News

Submit news

Send a chapter win, service project, or research highlight. Leadership can place it on the homepage, on MediLink social, or at an event. Send what actually happened. This site does not invent school names or fake news. A submission is not a guarantee of placement. Use medi.link.edu@gmail.com with a subject line that names the chapter and the work.
