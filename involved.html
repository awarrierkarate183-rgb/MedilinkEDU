Work with MediLink

This page is for sponsors, partners, and volunteers. A company should leave knowing how to give, which tier attends events, and how to offer shadowing. A volunteer should leave knowing how to sit on a panel or mentor a chapter. Students starting a school should use Start a Chapter, not this page.

Where it goes

Individual gifts fund chapters, competitions, and outreach. You should know where the money goes before you give: starter kits, event materials, service drives, and the work of keeping a two-founder network online. There is no separate individual tier list.
