Work with MediLink

This page is for sponsors, partners, and volunteers. A company should leave knowing how to give, which tier attends events, and how to offer shadowing. A volunteer should leave knowing how to sit on a panel or mentor a chapter. Students starting a school should use Start a Chapter, not this page.

01

How to sponsor

Individual gifts and company gifts both go to chapters, competitions, and outreach. You should know where the money goes before you give: starter kits, event materials, service drives, and the work of keeping a two-founder network online. Named corporate tiers live on the corporate page.

Attend National and State competitions

This perk is available only at the Impact Partner tier and above. Those sponsors can attend and be recognized in person, including the Apex networking fair in an Apex year. Community Ally and Health Advocate sponsors get the perk by moving to Impact Partner or Visionary Sponsor.
