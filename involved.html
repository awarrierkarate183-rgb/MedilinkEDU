Support

Work with MediLink

This page is for sponsors, partners, and volunteers. A company should leave knowing how to give, which tier attends events, and how to offer shadowing. A volunteer should leave knowing how to sit on a panel or mentor a chapter. Students starting a school should use Start a Chapter, not this page.

01 / Give

How to sponsor

Individual gifts and company gifts both go to chapters, competitions, and outreach.

Where it goes

Starter kits, event materials, service drives, and the work of keeping a two-founder network online.

How to give

Families, alumni, and students use the individual page. Companies choose Community Ally, Health Advocate, Impact Partner, or Visionary Sponsor. There is no separate individual tier list.

Sponsor as an individual See corporate tiers

Perk

Attend National and State competitions

This perk is available only at Impact Partner and above.

Who attends

Impact Partner and Visionary Sponsor can attend and be recognized in person, including the Apex networking fair in an Apex year.

How to get it

Community Ally and Health Advocate sponsors get the perk by moving to Impact Partner or Visionary Sponsor.
