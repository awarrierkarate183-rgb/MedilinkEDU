Attend National & State Competitions

Corporate sponsors at the Impact Partner tier and above can attend and be recognized at State and National MediLink competitions in person — including the biennial Apex networking fair in an Apex year. Community Ally and Health Advocate sponsors can unlock this benefit by upgrading to Impact Partner or Visionary Sponsor.

View Corporate Tiers

Shadowing & Internships

Clinical partners (hospitals and clinics), finance partners (insurers, healthcare finance firms, investors), and tech partners (health-tech companies) can offer shadow days and internships to MediLink members. These are coordinated through chapter advisors. Interested organizations should reach out through the Partner page so leadership can set up the relationship properly — including any waivers and school sign-off — before students are placed.
