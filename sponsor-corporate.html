Where corporate sponsorship goes

Corporate gifts fund chapter growth, competitions, outreach, and the work of keeping the network online. A named tier can also name a Nationals track or award. Naming does not change the three-lens method judges use. Community Ally and Health Advocate do not receive the in-person attend perk. Impact Partner and Visionary Sponsor do, including the Apex networking fair in an Apex year.

Chapter growth

Startup resources, advisor support, and student leadership training. Starter kits and first-semester agendas are part of this work.

Competition and learning

Event materials, research tools, and student awards across four annual competitions and the biennial Apex. Nationals remains the yearly flagship.

Community outreach

Service drives and local health-economics initiatives owned inside chapters by the Outreach and Service Lead.

Operations and reach

Website, communications, and network expansion. A two-founder network stays online because this work is funded.
