Chapter Support

Resources and direct help for chapters that already exist. New schools should start on Start a Chapter.

📋

Curriculum & Starter Kit

Need Track 1, meeting agendas, or the sample local case challenge? Chapter Support can resend the starter kit and walk officers through the four-track syllabus.

🏆

Competition Support

Prep help for Nationals, Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, Research Symposium, and, in an Apex year, the biennial summit. Dates and formats come from your state chapter board.

💬

Leadership Coaching

Chapter leaders can ask for help on officer roles, recruiting, and moving from Founding to Flagship-Eligible.

Chapter Support Areas

  • Activity planning: help structuring drives, local case challenges, and community outreach.
  • Curriculum delivery: support for Tracks 1-4 and officer ownership of the financial and technology lenses.
  • Competition preparation: Nationals uses the Regional to State to National ladder. The other three annual events do not.
  • Member recruitment: growing your chapter and engaging new students.
  • Community partnerships: connecting chapters with local organizations and resources.
  • Recognition: opportunities to spotlight your chapter's work on the MediLink site.
