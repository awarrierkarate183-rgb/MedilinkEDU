Chapter Support

Resources, guidance, and direct support for existing MediLink chapters to thrive, grow, and make a lasting impact.

📋

Curriculum & Starter Kit

Need Module 1, meeting agendas, or the sample local case challenge? Chapter Support can resend the starter kit and walk officers through the five-module sequence.

🏆

Competition Support

Get preparation resources and coaching for MediLink’s four national high school competitions — Nationals, Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium. Dates and formats are set each year by your state chapter board.

💬

Leadership Coaching

Chapter leaders can reach out for mentorship on officer roles, recruiting, and moving from Founding to Flagship-Eligible.

What We Support

Chapter Support Areas

  • Activity planning — help structuring drives, local case challenges, and community outreach.
  • Curriculum delivery — support for Modules 1–5 and officer ownership of the Cost and Code pillars.
  • Competition preparation — guidance on research, presentations, and the Regional → State → National ladder.
  • Member recruitment — strategies for growing your chapter and engaging new students.
  • Community partnerships — connecting chapters with local organizations and resources.
  • Recognition — opportunities to spotlight your chapter's work on the MediLink platform.
