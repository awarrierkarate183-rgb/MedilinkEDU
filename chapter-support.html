Chapter Support

Resources and direct help for chapters that already exist. New schools should start on Start a Chapter.

What we can send

Need Track 1, meeting agendas, or the sample local case challenge? Chapter Support can resend the starter kit and walk officers through the four-track syllabus.

What we help with

Prep help for Nationals, Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, Research Symposium, and, in an Apex year, the biennial summit. Dates and formats come from your state chapter board.

What we help with

Chapter leaders can ask for help on officer roles, recruiting, and moving from Founding to Flagship-Eligible.

Activity planning

Help structuring drives, local case challenges, and community outreach.

Curriculum delivery

Support for Tracks 1-4 and officer ownership of the financial and technology lenses.

Competition preparation

Nationals uses the Regional to State to National ladder. The other three annual events do not.

Member recruitment

Growing your chapter and engaging new students.

Community partnerships

Connecting chapters with local organizations and resources.

Recognition

Opportunities to spotlight your chapter's work on the MediLink site.
