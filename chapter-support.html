Chapter Support

Resources and direct help for chapters that already exist. New schools should start on Start a Chapter.

Kit

Curriculum and starter kit

Need Track 1, meeting agendas, or the sample local case challenge?

Chapter Support can resend the starter kit and walk officers through the four-track syllabus.

Compete

Competition support

Dates and formats come from your state chapter board.

Prep help for Nationals, Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, Research Symposium, and, in an Apex year, the biennial summit. Nationals uses the Regional to State to National ladder. The other three annual events do not.

Lead

Leadership coaching

Officer roles, recruiting, and the path to Flagship-Eligible.

Also: activity planning, curriculum delivery, member recruitment, community partnerships, and recognition on the MediLink site.

Request

Existing chapters can request help at any time

Ask for help with events or operations. New schools should not use this form. They should start a chapter.
