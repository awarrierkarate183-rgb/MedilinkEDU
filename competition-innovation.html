MediLink Innovation Challenge

A pitch competition for a health-tech product or digital-health concept. The entry point for tech-minded members.

What It Is

Students pitch an app, device concept, or platform idea that addresses a real gap in care, access, or health operations. Judges look at feasibility, impact, and a basic business case — not a finished product.

Who Can Participate

Open to high school students in grades 9–12 who are active members of a MediLink chapter.

What to Expect

A pitch-deck and live presentation format that advances Regional → State → National. Exact round length and materials are set each year by the state chapter board and shared through chapter leaders.

How to Register

Compete Through Your Chapter

Participation is through a MediLink chapter at your high school. Ask your chapter leader for this year’s Innovation Challenge timeline.
