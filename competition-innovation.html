MediLink Innovation Challenge

One annual pitch event. An app, a device concept, or a platform idea. Individual or teams of up to 3. A natural next step after Track 2. There is no regional or state qualifier.

What It Is

Students pitch an app, device concept, or platform idea that addresses a real gap in care, access, or health operations. Judges score feasibility, impact, and a basic business case. A finished product is not required.

Who Can Participate

Open to high school students in grades 9-12 who are active members of a MediLink chapter. Individual or teams of up to 3.

What to Expect

A single annual pitch-deck and live presentation. The state chapter board sets length and materials each year and shares them through chapter leaders. Top finishers earn Apex points on the one-time schedule.

Compete through your chapter

You enter through a MediLink chapter at your high school. Ask your chapter leader for this year's Innovation Challenge timeline.
