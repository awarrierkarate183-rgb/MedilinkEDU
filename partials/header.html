Competitions
Flagship and summit MediLink Nationals Yearly flagship. Teams of 3 to 4. Same Track 4 capstone case. Only event that climbs Regional to State to National. Intended National home: Charlotte. Nationals ladder Regional, then State, then National. The other three annual events do not have qualifier rounds. Dates come from state chapter boards. The MediLink Apex Every other year the National round is enlarged. Not a sixth case. Champions from the other four events are invited. Road to Apex standings live on this page.
One-time annual events Innovation Challenge One pitch per year. App, device concept, or platform idea. No regional or state qualifier. Individual or teams of up to 3. Policy Cup One brief and debate per year. Topics from the state chapter board. Individual or pairs. No ladder. Research Symposium One poster and talk per year. Lowest barrier of the five. Health economics or health tech. No ladder.
Structure Five competitions. Two structures. Three one-time annuals. Nationals on a ladder. Apex is a biennial enlargement of Nationals, not a sixth prompt. You compete through a chapter.

Get Involved
Give How to sponsor Individual gifts and company gifts both go to kits, events, outreach, and operations. No individual tier list. Impact Partner and above attend State and National in person. Give as an individual Families, alumni, and students. This page does not list individual donor dollar amounts. Named company levels live on the corporate page. Corporate tiers Community Ally, Health Advocate, Impact Partner, and Visionary Sponsor. Official benefits load from the board list. Amounts can be customized with leadership.
Work with us Partner Hospitals, insurers, and health-tech firms sit in the same three-lens framework. Leadership sets up each relationship before students are placed. Not a public job board. Volunteer Mentor, judge, or teach a module. Tell us your field. A two-hour workshop mapped to the syllabus is more useful than a generic pep talk. Submit news Send a chapter win that actually happened. Leadership decides whether it runs on the homepage, on MediLink social, or at an event. This site does not invent news.
