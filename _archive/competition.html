MediLink Nationals is the yearly flagship. Grades 9-12 in a Flagship-Eligible chapter, or a chapter on that path. Teams of 3 to 4. Every team works the same Track 4 capstone case for that year. Curriculum and competition stay linked because the case comes from Track 4.

Teams must cover clinical, financial, and technology. A Grand Prize still has to hold all three. Packets still use the internal names Care, Cost, Code. A year includes a written brief and video pitch, live regionals, a national final, and an awards evening. State chapter boards set dates. This page is the structure, not a calendar. Intended National home: Charlotte.

What this event is, and is not

  • It is the only event that climbs Regional to State to National.
  • It is not Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, or Research Symposium. Those are one-time annuals with no qualifier rounds.
  • In an Apex year this National round is enlarged. It is still Nationals. The Apex is not a sixth prompt with its own case.
  • You compete through a chapter. This site does not take independent entries.

Awards

Best Clinical Insight, Best Financial Model, Best Tech Innovation, Grand Prize. Sponsors can name a track or award. Naming does not change the method judges use.

Packets

Internal name Care, Cost, Code. Track 4 Module 4.3 is where case-team prep lives. Full packets wait behind roster approval. Login is not live yet.

Who may enter

Flagship-Eligible means the chapter has participated in a Nationals regional round and may send teams to Nationals. Chapters still on the path can use the regional round as part of becoming Flagship-Eligible.

Points

Nationals points add as a team advances. That is why Nationals is worth more on the Road to Apex. There is no extra multiplier. Numbers come from a board-maintained ledger, not live scores.

Only Nationals uses this ladder. Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium do not have regional or state qualifier rounds. Dates come from state chapter boards. Ask your chapter leader. This site is not a calendar.

Three phases in a Nationals year

  • Phase 1: Case Release. Remote written brief plus a short video pitch, built from that year's Track 4 capstone.
  • Phase 2: Regional Rounds. Live presentation to judges from hospitals, finance firms, and health-tech.
  • Phase 3: National Finals. Live final and awards evening. Intended home: Charlotte.

Level 1 / Regional

First Nationals round, run at or near chapters. Participating here is part of the path to Flagship-Eligible. Judges should still sit in the three lenses: clinic, money, and tech.

Level 2 / State

The state chapter board coordinates this round. North Carolina and Georgia are the states currently listed on the chapter map. Dates still come from those boards, not from this page.

Level 3 / National

State qualifiers compete nationally. In an Apex year, this level is the summit. Same case, enlarged year. Champions from the other three annuals can be invited then.

What judges look for

Can you name the user, the gap, and a path to pay for it. Outcome over novelty. A wireframe or workflow is honest. A fake finished app is not.

Format

  • Grades 9-12 in an active chapter.
  • One annual poster plus an oral presentation.
  • Question, method, and finding have to be on the poster.
  • Track 4 Module 4.3 is the public home for poster prep.

What belongs here

A real question in health economics or health tech, a method a high school student can actually run, and a finding the student can defend in a short talk.

What does not

A literature dump with no question. A Nationals case restated as a poster. A topic this site invented. Leadership does not write student research on this page.

Every two years the National round is enlarged. Top performers from Nationals plus champions from Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium are invited. Championship round across all four disciplines. Sponsor networking fair. Keynotes from medicine, finance, and health-tech. Awards gala.

The Apex is not a sixth case and not a sixth competition with its own prompt. Nationals stays the yearly flagship. In an Apex year you are still looking at Nationals, enlarged. A new two-year cycle starts at zero after each Apex. Qualification also uses the Road to Apex ledger.

What happens in an Apex year

  • Same Track 4 capstone case as that year's Nationals.
  • Invited champions from the three one-time annuals join the National round.
  • Sponsor networking fair. Impact Partner and Visionary Sponsor can attend the fair in person.
  • Keynotes and an awards gala sit on top of the usual National final, they do not replace it.

Who is invited

Top Nationals performers and champions from Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium. You still compete through a chapter.
