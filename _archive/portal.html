Member Portal

Approved members use a registered MediLink login to open full lessons, labs, and competition resources. The public syllabus stays on the Curriculum page. Login is not live yet. Do not expect a working password box on this page.

Your school chapter reaches Founding status. The chapter receives a unique QR code. You scan that code and complete a short form already tied to that chapter. Officers or the advisor approve the roster. You receive a MediLink login that opens this portal. That last step is not built yet. A real portal needs an auth provider and a database. That is its own project phase.

Members do not create a generic account on this website. Do not expect a working password box on this page. Until login is live, ask your chapter leader for curriculum files. New schools should start a chapter, not email for a dump of lessons.

What is public vs gated

  • Public: track titles, module titles, and one-line descriptions on Curriculum.
  • Gated: full lessons, slide decks, lab worksheets, and competition packets.
  • Scoped: access is tied to a real chapter roster, not a public sign-up.
  • Not yet: the login itself. Email about access if you are already on a roster and cannot find files.
