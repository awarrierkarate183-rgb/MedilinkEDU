Officer structure

Every chapter uses the same five offices so members have real titles and so the three-lens framework has owners besides the two people who founded MediLink.

President

Sets the chapter agenda, represents the chapter, and keeps officers aligned.

VP of Operations

Runs meetings, logistics, and the day-to-day calendar.

Finance / Treasury Lead

Owns the financial lens inside the chapter: budgets, Track 3 labs, and finance workshops.

Technology Lead

Owns the technology lens: Track 2 content and the chapter's digital projects.

Outreach & Service Lead

Runs service, recruiting, and local partnerships.

How chapters level up

Status is earned by what the chapter has actually done, not by a label we put on the website in advance.

Status 1

Founding Chapter

Chartered, has officers, and is running meetings.

Status 2

Established Chapter

Has run at least one internal event or case challenge, and has 10+ active members.

Status 3

Flagship-Eligible Chapter

Has participated in a Nationals regional round and may send teams to Nationals.

Reactivate a Chapter

Chapters that have gone inactive (no officers, no meetings, or lapsed membership) can be reactivated rather than started from scratch. Reactivation keeps the chapter's history and name. What you need is new student leadership ready to step up.
