Start a Chapter

Bring MediLink to your high school. New chapters get a consistent officer structure, a starter kit, and a path from Founding to Flagship-Eligible. The first semester is not invented from scratch. You receive a charter template, the first three meeting agendas and decks, Track 1 Module 1.1 ready to teach, and a sample local case challenge. Inactive chapters can be reactivated rather than started over. This page is for students launching a school. Sponsors and volunteers should use Get Involved.

Three steps

Apply. Set up. Make an impact.

Submit the chapter application with your school name and why you want to start. We reply with the next steps. Then fill the five officer roles, receive the starter kit, and plan your first meetings from the pre-built agendas. In the first semester, run Track 1, host a local case challenge, and work toward Established and Flagship-Eligible status.

The form link is set in data/forms.config.js when it is ready. If the button alerts you, the URL has not been pasted yet. Email medi.link.edu@gmail.com if you need a path while the form is offline.

Officers

The same five offices in every chapter

So the three-lens framework has owners besides the two people who founded MediLink. Every chapter uses this structure. Do not invent extra officer titles that hide the work. The Finance Lead owns money. The Technology Lead owns systems. Outreach owns service. President and VP of Operations keep the chapter running.

President

Sets the chapter agenda, represents the chapter, and keeps officers aligned. The President does not replace the other four offices. The method needs owners, not a single founder doing every lens.

VP of Operations

Runs meetings, logistics, and the day-to-day calendar. First-semester agendas come in the starter kit. The VP's job is to run them, then build the rest of the year on that rhythm.

Finance / Treasury Lead

Owns the financial lens: budgets, Track 3 labs, and finance workshops. In Nationals this maps to the Cost award, Best Financial Model.

Technology Lead

Owns the technology lens: Track 2 content and the chapter's digital projects. In Nationals this maps to the Code award, Best Tech Innovation. Innovation Challenge is a separate annual pitch.

Outreach and Service Lead

Runs service, recruiting, and local partnerships. Homepage news and community work go through this office. Give-back is a stated aim, not an extra club.

Starter kit

Charter template. First 3 meeting agendas and decks. Track 1 Module 1.1 ready to teach. A sample local case challenge for the first semester. Chapter Support can resend the kit if it is lost.

Roster

How members join your chapter

Once a chapter is Founding, it receives a unique QR code tied to that roster. Members do not create a generic account on this website. They scan the chapter code, complete a short form already linked to that school, and wait for officer or advisor approval. Approved members are supposed to receive a MediLink login that opens the Member Portal. Login is not live yet. Until it is, officers share curriculum files directly.

01 Founding status

Officers are in place and the chapter is chartered. The board can record the chapter on the map. School names appear when that record exists, not before.

02 Chapter QR

A unique code for flyers, emails, and the school. It is tied to this roster. Do not reuse another chapter's code.

03 Scan and register

The form is already linked to this chapter's roster. Students at that school complete it. This is not a public join button on the homepage.

04 Approval, then portal

Officers or the advisor confirm real students at that school. Full lessons, labs, and competition prep wait behind that approval. The public syllabus stays open to everyone.

Status

How chapters level up

Status is earned by what the chapter has actually done. The Chapters page shows a recorded status and this cycle's Apex points when the board has entered them. Full rankings live on Road to Apex. Labels are not assigned for marketing.

Founding Chapter

Chartered, has officers, and is running meetings. Receives the QR code. Can appear on the map once the board records the school.

Established Chapter

Has run at least one internal event or case challenge, and has 10 or more active members. Still not automatically Flagship-Eligible.

Flagship-Eligible Chapter

Has participated in a Nationals regional round and may send teams to Nationals. This is the path to the yearly flagship. The three one-time annual events do not require this label.

Earned, not assigned

Labels track work already done. A chapter that goes inactive can be reactivated and keep its history and name if new student leadership steps up.

Return

Reactivate a chapter

Chapters that have gone inactive (no officers, no meetings, or lapsed membership) can be reactivated rather than started from scratch. Reactivation keeps the chapter's history and name. What you need is new student leadership ready to step up. Email medi.link.edu@gmail.com with the subject Chapter Reactivation. Existing chapters that need the kit or coaching should use Chapter Support instead.
