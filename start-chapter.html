Leadership

Officer structure

Every chapter uses the same five offices so members have real titles and so Care / Cost / Code has owners — not only the two people who founded MediLink.

President

Sets the chapter agenda, represents the chapter, and keeps officers aligned.

VP of Operations

Runs meetings, logistics, and the day-to-day calendar.

Finance / Treasury Lead

Owns the Cost pillar inside the chapter — budgets, Module 4 labs, and finance workshops.

Technology Lead

Owns the Code pillar — Module 2 content and the chapter’s digital projects.

Outreach & Service Lead

Runs service, recruiting, and local partnerships.

Chapter Status

How chapters level up

Status is earned by what the chapter has actually done — not by a label we put on the website in advance.

Status 1

Founding Chapter

Chartered, has officers, and is running meetings.

Status 2

Established Chapter

Has run at least one internal event or case challenge, and has 10+ active members.

Status 3

Flagship-Eligible Chapter

Has participated in a regional competition and may send teams to Nationals.
