Start a Chapter

Bring MediLink to your high school. New chapters get five offices, a starter kit, and a path from Founding to Flagship-Eligible. Sponsors and volunteers should use Get Involved. Open a file for steps, officers, the QR roster, and status.

Submit the chapter application with your school name and why you want to start. Fill the five officer roles, receive the starter kit, and plan first meetings from the pre-built agendas. Run Track 1, host a local case challenge, and work toward Established and Flagship-Eligible. We do not invent school listings on the map until the board records the chapter.

Three steps, in order

  • Apply. School name, student leadership, and a reason that is not a copied slogan.
  • Set up. Five offices, starter kit, first 3 agendas, Track 1 Module 1.1, sample local case challenge. Founding status, then a unique QR.
  • Make an impact. Meetings running, an internal event or case challenge, then a Nationals regional round on the path to Flagship-Eligible.

Sponsors and volunteers should use Get Involved, not this form. Inactive chapters that need new student leadership should reactivate, not start over. Existing officers who need the kit should use Chapter Support.

The same five offices exist in every chapter so the three-lens framework has owners besides the founders. Volunteers back these offices. They do not replace them. A title without work behind it does not count.

President

Sets the agenda, represents the chapter, and keeps officers aligned. Owns the public face of the chapter to the school and to MediLink leadership.

VP of Operations

Runs meetings, logistics, and the day-to-day calendar from the starter agendas. First 3 meeting decks are already in the kit.

Finance / Treasury Lead

Owns the financial lens: budgets, Track 3 labs, finance workshops. Cost-lens partners sit with this office, not instead of it.

Technology Lead

Owns the technology lens: Track 2 and digital projects. Outcome over novelty. A finished product is not required.

Outreach and Service Lead

Service, recruiting, and local partnerships. Homepage news goes through this office. Submit News still waits for work that actually happened.

Starter kit

Charter template. First 3 meeting agendas and decks. Track 1 Module 1.1. Sample local case challenge. Full Track 2-4 lessons still wait behind roster approval.

What the QR is for

  • It is tied to that school's roster, not a public sign-up on medilink.edu.
  • It is issued after Founding, not before the chapter exists.
  • Approval still belongs to officers or the advisor. A scan is not automatic membership.
  • Full lessons, labs, and packets wait behind that approval.
Member Portal Public syllabus

Founding, Established, Flagship-Eligible. Labels track work already done. They are not marketing badges and they are not for sale. The three one-time annual events do not require Flagship-Eligible. Nationals does, or a chapter on that path using the regional round to get there.

Founding

Chartered, officers in place, meetings running. Receives the QR code. The map does not list a school until the board records it.

Established

At least one internal event or case challenge, and 10 or more active members. This is still chapter work, not Nationals yet.

Flagship-Eligible

Has participated in a Nationals regional round and may send teams to Nationals. Teams of 3 to 4. Same Track 4 capstone.

Reactivate a chapter

Chapters that have gone inactive can be reactivated rather than started from scratch. Reactivation keeps the chapter's history and name. What you need is new student leadership ready to step up. Existing officers who need the kit should use Chapter Support. Brand new schools should use the application at the top of this page, not this email.

A reactivated chapter still uses the same five offices, the same public syllabus, and the same competition calendar. Status is earned again from the work in front of you. Labels are not restored as a courtesy badge.
