Member Portal

Approved members use a registered MediLink login to open full lessons, labs, and competition resources. The public syllabus stays on the Curriculum page.

How you get in

Access is scoped to a real chapter roster — not a generic sign-up on this website.

  1. Your school chapter reaches Founding status (chartered, officers in place).
  2. The chapter receives a unique QR code for flyers, emails, and the school.
  3. You scan that code and complete a short form already tied to that chapter.
  4. Chapter officers or the advisor approve the roster.
  5. You receive a MediLink login that unlocks this portal.
