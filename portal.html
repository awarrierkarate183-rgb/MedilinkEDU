Access

How you get in

Access is scoped to a real chapter roster, not a generic sign-up on this website.

Your school chapter reaches Founding status (chartered, officers in place). The chapter receives a unique QR code. You scan that code and complete a short form already tied to that chapter. Officers or the advisor approve the roster. You receive a MediLink login that opens this portal.
