Partner With Us

Hospitals, insurers, and health-tech firms sit in the same three-lens framework students use. Schools and community groups can join that pipeline too. Leadership sets up each relationship before students are placed. This is not a public job board. Open a file.

Clinical partners help students name the patient and the gap, not practice medicine from a club meeting. Care-lens judges for Nationals regional rounds are a strong fit.

Also biotech investors. Track 3 is the syllabus home for this work. A Nationals team that cannot say who pays has not finished the financial lens.

Track 2 and Innovation Challenge are the student-facing homes for this work. Outcome over novelty. A finished product is not required of student teams.

High schools and districts can help a chapter form and host a local case challenge or a Nationals regional round. Libraries and local groups can collaborate on outreach. Shadowing and internships are offered through clinical, finance, and tech partners and coordinated through chapter advisors. We do not post legal requirements on this site.
