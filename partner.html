Finance

Insurers, healthcare finance, consulting, and biotech investors. Mentorship, Cost-lens judges, and finance workshops.

Technology

Health-tech startups and digital health companies. Internships, Code-lens judges, and product demos.

Placement

Leadership sets up each relationship before students are placed. We do not post legal requirements on this site.
