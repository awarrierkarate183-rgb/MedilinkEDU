How placement works

Offered through clinical, finance, and tech partners and coordinated through chapter advisors. Leadership sets up each relationship before students are placed. We do not post legal requirements on this site.

Judge

Sit on a judge panel for a Nationals regional or national round, or for one of the three one-time annual events.

Teach

Host a guest workshop that maps to a curriculum module.

Sponsor

Sponsor a Nationals award, a competition track, or the biennial Apex networking fair. Full tier benefits live on the corporate sponsor page.

Place and host

Offer shadow days or internships coordinated through chapter advisors. Donate resources or venue space for MediLink events and drives. Share MediLink with your own network and channels.

Who attends

Every other year, the National round becomes the MediLink Apex. The dedicated sponsor networking fair is where partners get face time with students. Impact Partner and Visionary Sponsor are the tiers that attend State and National events in person.
