Partner With Us

Hospitals, insurers, and health-tech firms sit in the same three-lens framework students use. Schools and community groups can join that pipeline too. Leadership sets up each relationship before students are placed. We do not post legal requirements on this site. Shadowing and internships are not a public job board. A useful workshop is mapped to a syllabus module. A useful judge panel covers clinic, money, and tech the same way Nationals is judged.

Three worlds

Where partners sit

Students are trained to hold clinic, money, and technology on one case. Partners are invited into that same map. A hospital that only wants a career day, with no clinical content a chapter can use, is a weaker fit than a hospital that will host a shadow day or send a Care-lens judge. The same test applies to insurers and health-tech firms.

Clinical

Hospitals, clinics, and physicians. Shadow days, guest speakers, and Care-lens judges for Nationals regional rounds. Clinical partners help students name the patient and the gap, not practice medicine from a club meeting.

Finance

Insurers, healthcare finance, consulting, and biotech investors. Mentorship, Cost-lens judges, and finance workshops. Track 3 is the syllabus home for this work.

Technology

Health-tech startups and digital health companies. Internships, Code-lens judges, and product demos. Track 2 and Innovation Challenge are the student-facing homes for this work.

Placement

Leadership sets up each relationship before students are placed. We do not post legal requirements on this site. Ask us what a given placement requires. Do not assume a public listing.

Also

Schools, community, and placement

High schools and districts can help a chapter form and host a local case challenge or a Nationals regional round. Libraries, community centers, and local groups can collaborate on outreach drives and health-economics education. Shadowing and internships are offered through clinical, finance, and tech partners and coordinated through chapter advisors.

School partners

Help a chapter form. Host a local case challenge or Nationals regional round. Provide an advisor so the five student offices are not working without an adult in the building.

Community partners

Outreach drives and health-economics education. The Outreach and Service Lead owns this inside the chapter. Homepage news can feature the work after leadership reviews it.

Shadowing and internships

Offered through clinical, finance, and tech partners and coordinated through chapter advisors. Not posted as open roles on this website.
