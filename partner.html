Partner With Us

MediLink’s partner pipeline matches the three lenses: clinical partners, finance partners, and tech partners — plus schools and community groups.

Partner Pipeline

Clinical, Finance, and Tech

🏥

Clinical partners

Hospitals, clinics, and physicians — shadow days, guest speakers, and Care-lens judges for regional rounds.

🏦

Finance partners

Health insurers, healthcare finance and consulting firms, and biotech investors — mentorship, Cost-lens judges, and finance workshops.

💻

Tech partners

Health-tech startups and digital health companies — internships, Code-lens judges, and product demos.

🏫

School Partners

High schools and districts can work with MediLink to bring competitions into their communities and support student chapter formation.

🤝

Community Partners

Libraries, community centers, and local groups can collaborate on outreach drives and health-economics education initiatives.

💼

Shadowing & Internships

Offered through clinical, finance, and tech partners and coordinated through chapter advisors. Leadership sets up each relationship before students are placed — we do not post legal requirements on this site.

What Partnerships Look Like

Ways to Collaborate

  • Sit on a judge panel for Regional or National rounds (Care, Cost, or Code).
  • Host a guest workshop that maps to a curriculum module.
  • Sponsor a Nationals award or a competition track.
  • Offer shadow days or internships coordinated through chapter advisors.
  • Donate resources or venue space for MediLink events and drives.
  • Amplify MediLink's mission through your own network and communications channels.
