Insurers, healthcare finance, consulting, and biotech investors. Mentorship, Cost-lens judges, and Track 3 workshops. Track 3 is the syllabus home for this work. A Nationals team that cannot say who pays has not finished the financial lens.

Unit economics in health are not the same as a typical consumer product. A useful workshop covers premiums, billing, grants, or a one-page budget, not a generic markets talk.
