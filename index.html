02 / Compete

Five events. Two structures.

One-time annual

Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, Research Symposium. Each once a year. No regional or state qualifier.

Nationals

Yearly flagship. Regional to State to National. Case from that year's Track 4 capstone. Teams of 3 to 4.

Apex

Every other year the National round becomes the summit. Not a sixth case. Top performers from the other four are invited.

Dates

State boards set dates through chapter leaders. This site is the structure, not a calendar.
