We train students to solve healthcare the way real systems are solved.

A student-founded high school network in Charlotte. Members learn through chapters, a four-track syllabus, and five competitions that treat medicine, money, and technology as one problem.

01 / Framework

One problem. Three lenses.

Real health systems do not split into a clinic track, a finance track, and a tech track. A hospital bill, a coverage rule, and a records system sit on the same case. MediLink trains chapters to work that way from the first meeting.

The three-lens framework is the method. About is the page that opens each lens and shows how they lock together. Case packets still use the internal name Care, Cost, Code.
