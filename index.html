The Framework

Every activity uses three lenses at once

That is what makes MediLink different from a medicine-only club or a business-only club. Clinical understanding, financial reasoning, and technology land on the same problem.

Care

Clinical lens

What’s the actual health problem? Who’s affected? This is the medicine side — the patient, the condition, and the clinical need.

Cost

Financial lens

Who pays for it? What does it cost? Is a solution sustainable, insurable, or fundable? This is the money side of healthcare.

Code

Systems lens

How could data, software, devices, or infrastructure change access or delivery? This is the technology and systems side.
