01 / Method

One problem. Three lenses.

A hospital bill, a coverage rule, and a records system sit on the same case.

Real health systems do not split into a clinic track, a finance track, and a tech track. MediLink trains chapters to hold all three from the first meeting.

The three-lens framework is the method. About is the page that opens each lens and shows how they lock together. Case packets still use the internal name Care, Cost, Code.

02 / Compete

Five events. Two structures.

Only Nationals climbs a ladder. The Apex is the biennial summit of the other four.

Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium each run once a year, with no regional or state qualifier. Nationals is the yearly flagship: Regional to State to National, on a case drawn from that year's Track 4 capstone.

Every other year that National round becomes the Apex. It is not a sixth standalone case. Top performers from the other four events are invited into an enlarged National summit.

03 / Belong

Chapters earn their status.

Founding, Established, then Flagship-Eligible. Labels follow work that actually happened.

Find a chapter or start one. Founding chapters get a QR code tied to their roster, then a path from first meetings to Nationals eligibility. Listings currently cover North Carolina and Georgia. School arrays stay empty until the board records real chapters. Do not invent names to fill the map.

04 / Place

Started in Charlotte. Built to travel.

Two high school students founded MediLink because existing clubs split the work.

One club trains clinical skill. Another trains business. A third treats technology as a side interest. Health systems do not work that way. The network is still young. What is public is the method, the syllabus, and the competition calendar, not a catalog of invented wins.
