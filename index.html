Method

One problem. Three lenses.

Open a lens. About is the only page that explains each one in full. Case packets still use the internal name Care, Cost, Code.

Skip this lens and a team writes a business plan for a problem it cannot describe. In Nationals the Care award is Best Clinical Insight.

Read the clinical lens

If the money path fails, the clinical idea stays on the page. The chapter Finance Lead owns Track 3. In Nationals the Cost award is Best Financial Model.

Read the financial lens

The Technology Lead owns Track 2. In Nationals the Code award is Best Tech Innovation. A finished product is not required. A change in access is.

Compete

Five events. Two structures.

Three one-time annual events. Nationals is the only Regional to State to National ladder. The Apex is a biennial enlargement of that National round, not a sixth case. Click a file.

Grades 9-12 in a Flagship-Eligible chapter, or a chapter on that path. Awards: Best Clinical Insight, Best Financial Model, Best Tech Innovation, Grand Prize. Intended National home: Charlotte. Dates come from state boards.

Full Nationals page

App, device concept, or platform idea. Feasibility, impact, basic business case. Finished product not required. Individual or teams of up to 3. Apex points on the one-time schedule.

Full event

Research a live issue, write a brief, argue a concrete reform. Individual or pairs. No qualifier rounds. Apex points on the one-time schedule.
