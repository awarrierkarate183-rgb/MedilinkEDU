Conferences & Events

The Three-Tier Event Ladder

Every MediLink competition feeds the same national brand through Regional, State, and National events. This page describes the structure — not a specific year’s calendar.

The specific dates, formats, and requirements for each level are set each year by the state chapter board and communicated to members through their individual chapter leaders. This page describes the structure — check with your chapter leader for what's happening this year.

How Events Are Organized

Regional, State, and National

Level 1

Regional Events

The first competitive level, run at or near individual chapters, open to all active members. This is where most students compete for the first time.

Level 2

State Events

Top performers from regional events advance. State events are run at the state level and coordinated by the state chapter board.

Level 3

National Events

The top state qualifiers compete nationally. For MediLink Nationals, this culminates in the awards gala — the signature gathering of the year.

What Runs on This Ladder

Four Competitions, One Structure

MediLink Nationals, the Innovation Challenge, the Policy Cup, and the Research Symposium all use this same Regional → State → National path.
