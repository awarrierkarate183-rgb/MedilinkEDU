Where your support goes

Individual gifts fund the same work as company gifts: chapter growth, competition events, community outreach, and student leadership training. They do not purchase a named corporate attend perk. Impact Partner and Visionary Sponsor are company levels. If you want to give as a company, use the corporate page.

The work

Starter kits, event materials, service drives, and the work of keeping the network online. A gift does not buy a student placement. Shadowing still runs through partners and advisors.

Who this page is for

Families, alumni, and students. Community Ally, Health Advocate, Impact Partner, and Visionary Sponsor are the named company levels. School and community groups can run a fundraiser with MediLink. Email us to set one up.
