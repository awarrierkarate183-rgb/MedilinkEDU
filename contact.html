How do I start a chapter?

Apply through our form and we will walk you through every step of launching a MediLink chapter at your high school. Inactive chapters can be reactivated instead of started from scratch. The Chapters map only lists schools the board has recorded.

Learn about chapters

How can I volunteer?

We welcome mentors, advisors, and supporters. Tell us your field so we can match you to a chapter, a panel, or a workshop mapped to a syllabus module. Visit the volunteer page to apply.

Volunteer information

How do I partner?

Organizations can explore partnership, shadowing, and internships. Shadowing is arranged through partners and chapter advisors, not a public job board. Clinical, finance, and tech partners sit in the same three-lens framework students use.
