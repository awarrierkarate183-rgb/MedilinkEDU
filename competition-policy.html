MediLink Policy Cup

A policy brief and advocacy competition where students argue for a real fix to a health-economics problem.

What It Is

Competitors research a live issue — insurance access, rural care deserts, drug pricing, and similar challenges — then write a brief and argue for a concrete reform. The format is debate and presentation, not a written exam.

Who Can Participate

Open to high school students in grades 9–12 who are active members of a MediLink chapter.

What to Expect

A policy brief plus live advocacy, advancing Regional → State → National. State chapter boards set prompts, round format, and requirements each year.

How to Register

Compete Through Your Chapter

Participation is through a MediLink chapter at your high school. Ask your chapter leader for this year’s Policy Cup timeline.
