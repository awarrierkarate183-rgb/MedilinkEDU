MediLink Policy Cup

One annual brief and debate. Individual or pairs. Topics come from the state chapter board: drug pricing, rural access, insurance reform, and similar. Volunteer healthcare, finance, and policy professionals judge. There is no regional or state qualifier.

What It Is

Competitors research a live issue, then write a brief and argue for a concrete reform. The format is debate and presentation, not a written exam. Track 1 and Track 3 are the usual prep.

Who Can Participate

Open to high school students in grades 9-12 who are active members of a MediLink chapter. Individual or pairs.

What to Expect

A single annual policy brief plus live advocacy. State chapter boards set the prompt, round format, and requirements each year. Top finishers earn Apex points on the one-time schedule.

Compete through your chapter

You enter through a MediLink chapter at your high school. Ask your chapter leader for this year's Policy Cup timeline.
