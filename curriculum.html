1.2 The Cost of Care

Why healthcare is expensive: administrative costs, drug pricing, and hospital billing.

1.3 Access and Disparities

Rural care deserts, uninsured populations, and global health gaps.

Electronic health records, telehealth, and patient portals.

2.2 Data and AI in Medicine

Diagnostics, imaging, and predictive tools, explained conceptually.

How monitoring technology is changing care delivery.

Lab

3.1 Reading a Budget

Revenue, expenses, and break-even basics.

3.2 Funding a Health Idea

Grants, investors, and nonprofit vs. for-profit funding paths.

3.3 Measuring Impact and ROI

Why return on investment is measured differently in health than in typical business.

Lab

Build a one-page budget and funding plan for a mock community health program.

One case run through the three-lens framework. Packets still use the internal name Care, Cost, Code.

4.2 Case Analysis Practice

Guided practice on past-style cases.
