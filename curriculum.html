Learn / Syllabus

Semester curriculum

Four tracks. Twelve modules. Any high school chapter can run this. Titles and one-line descriptions are public. Full lessons and labs wait until a chapter approves you.

Track 1

Foundations of Health Economics

How care is paid for, why it costs what it costs, and who is left out.

1.1 How Healthcare Gets Paid For. Insurance basics, premiums, deductibles, and employer vs. government coverage.

1.2 The Cost of Care. Why healthcare is expensive: administrative costs, drug pricing, and hospital billing.

1.3 Access and Disparities. Rural care deserts, uninsured populations, and global health gaps.

Hands-on lab: Build a basic insurance-comparison worksheet for a hypothetical family.

Track 2

Health Technology and Systems

Records, data, devices. No coding background required.

2.1 Digital Health 101. Electronic health records, telehealth, and patient portals.

2.2 Data and AI in Medicine. Diagnostics, imaging, and predictive tools, explained conceptually.

2.3 Medical Devices and Wearables. How monitoring technology is changing care delivery.

Hands-on lab: Wireframe one feature of a telehealth app.

Track 3

Financial Modeling for Health Ventures

Budgets, funding paths, and why ROI looks different in health.

3.1 Reading a Budget. Revenue, expenses, and break-even basics.

3.2 Funding a Health Idea. Grants, investors, and nonprofit vs. for-profit funding paths.

3.3 Measuring Impact and ROI. Why return on investment is measured differently in health than in typical business.

Hands-on lab: Build a one-page budget and funding plan for a mock community health program.

Track 4

Applied Capstone and Competition Prep

The yearly capstone becomes that year's Nationals case.

4.1 Applying the Framework. One case run through the three-lens framework. Packets still use the internal name Care, Cost, Code.

4.2 Case Analysis Practice. Guided practice on past-style cases.

4.3 Competition-Specific Prep. Pitch prep (Innovation Challenge), brief-writing (Policy Cup), poster prep (Research Symposium), and case-team prep (Nationals).

Hands-on lab: A full mock case run-through, in teams.
