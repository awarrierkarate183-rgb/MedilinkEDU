For Chapters

Semester Curriculum

A five-module base sequence any high school chapter can run. Finance Lead and Technology Lead officers own the content for their pillars so the work is not only on the founding team.

1

What Is Health Economics?

Intro to how healthcare gets paid for: insurance basics, out-of-pocket cost, and why care is expensive. This module ships with the Chapter Starter Kit.

2

Health Tech 101

Telehealth, digital health records, wearables, and AI in diagnostics — what’s actually being used today. Technology Lead typically owns this module.

3

Access & Disparities

Case studies on who lacks access and why — rural areas, uninsured populations, and global health gaps.

4

Build a Plan

Hands-on lab: teams design a mini clinic or program and build a basic budget and funding plan. This is where finance becomes tangible. Finance/Treasury Lead typically owns this module.

5

Competition Prep

Teaches the Care / Cost / Code framework directly and preps students for Nationals, the Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium.

How Chapters Use This

Built to run locally first

New chapters receive Module 1 in the starter kit. Established chapters work through the full sequence and use Module 5 before sending teams to Regional events. Slide decks and agendas are part of the kit — request them through Chapter Support if your files are missing.
