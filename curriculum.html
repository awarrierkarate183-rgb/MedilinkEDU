Semester curriculum

Four tracks. Twelve modules. Any high school chapter can run this. Titles and one-line descriptions are public. Full lessons and labs wait until a chapter approves you.

Track 1. Foundations of Health Economics

1.1

How Healthcare Gets Paid For

Insurance basics, premiums, deductibles, and employer vs. government coverage.

1.2

The Cost of Care

Why healthcare is expensive: administrative costs, drug pricing, and hospital billing.

1.3

Access & Disparities

Rural care deserts, uninsured populations, and global health gaps.

Hands-on lab: Build a basic insurance-comparison worksheet for a hypothetical family.

Track 2. Health Technology & Systems

2.1

Digital Health 101

Electronic health records, telehealth, and patient portals.

2.2

Data & AI in Medicine

Diagnostics, imaging, and predictive tools, explained conceptually. No coding background required.

2.3

Medical Devices & Wearables

How monitoring technology is changing care delivery.

Hands-on lab: Wireframe one feature of a telehealth app.

Track 3. Financial Modeling for Health Ventures

3.1

Reading a Budget

Revenue, expenses, and break-even basics.

3.2

Funding a Health Idea

Grants, investors, and nonprofit vs. for-profit funding paths.

3.3

Measuring Impact & ROI

Why return on investment is measured differently in health than in typical business.

Hands-on lab: Build a one-page budget and funding plan for a mock community health program.

Track 4. Applied Capstone & Competition Prep

4.1

Applying the Framework

One case run through the three-lens framework. Packets still use the internal name Care, Cost, Code.

4.2

Case Analysis Practice

Guided practice on past-style cases.

4.3

Competition-Specific Prep

Pitch prep (Innovation Challenge), brief-writing (Policy Cup), poster prep (Research Symposium), and case-team prep (Nationals).

Hands-on lab: A full mock case run-through, in teams. Each year's Track 4 capstone becomes the basis for that year's MediLink Nationals case.
