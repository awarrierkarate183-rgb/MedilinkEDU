Volunteer

Become a Volunteer

Mentor students, sit on a judge panel, or help a chapter run meetings. Tell us your field so we can match you. A two-hour workshop mapped to a syllabus module is more useful than a generic pep talk. Most chapters need one reliable adult besides the school advisor. Volunteers back the five student offices. They do not replace them. Leadership still sets up partner placements before students are sent into a clinical, finance, or tech setting.

Work

How volunteers help

Guide researchers and chapter officers in healthcare, economics, policy, or health tech. Help chapters plan events, organize drives, and enter competitions. Nationals needs judges who can score clinic, money, and tech. Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium need coaches who understand those formats. Those three events have no regional ladder. Nationals is the only climb.

Mentor

Guide researchers and chapter officers. Research Symposium is often the lowest barrier for a student who needs one adult who will read a draft. Do not promise publication. Promise a careful read.

Chapter

Most chapters need one reliable adult besides the school advisor. Help plan events, organize drives, and enter competitions. Map the workshop to Track 1, 2, 3, or 4 so officers can place it on the calendar.

Roles

Ways to contribute

Pick a role that matches your field. The apply card is last because the form still has to know what you can actually do. If the apply button alerts you, the Google Form URL is not in data/forms.config.js yet. Email medi.link.edu@gmail.com in the meantime.

Academic mentor

Guide research projects and student publications. Health economics or health tech. Research Symposium is the annual home for that work.

Competition advisor

Help chapters prepare for Nationals or one of the three one-time annual events. Know which event has a ladder. Only Nationals does.

Outreach coordinator

Assist with community service drives. Work with the chapter Outreach and Service Lead, not around them.

Guest speaker

Share your professional perspective with students. Map it to a module: payment, records, devices, budgets, or case work.

Community liaison

Connect MediLink chapters with local organizations and resources. Leadership still has to approve placements.

Apply

Fill out the volunteer form. Tell us your background and how you want to help. Field first, then availability, then whether you can judge, teach, or mentor.
