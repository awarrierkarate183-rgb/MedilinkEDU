Share Your Expertise

Give a guest talk or advise on outreach. A two-hour workshop mapped to a syllabus module is more useful than a generic pep talk.

Ways to Contribute

  • Academic mentor: guide research projects and student publications.
  • Competition advisor: help chapters prepare for Nationals or one of the three one-time annual events.
  • Outreach coordinator: assist with community service drives.
  • Guest speaker: share your professional perspective with students.
  • Community liaison: connect MediLink chapters with local organizations and resources.
