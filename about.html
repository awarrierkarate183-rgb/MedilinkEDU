Who we are

Two high school students in Charlotte, North Carolina started MediLink because existing clubs split the work. One club trains clinical skill. Another trains business. A third treats technology as a side interest. Health systems do not work that way. We ask students to hold all three on one case.

The work

Members learn how real health systems work: a clinical problem, a money problem, and a technology problem at once. Chapters share one officer structure, one public syllabus, and one competition calendar.

What it is not

The work is chapter meetings, four tracks, twelve modules, and five competitions. Nationals is the yearly flagship. The Apex is the biennial upgrade of that National round.

The sentence we keep

To train the next generation of healthcare problem-solvers by combining clinical understanding, financial reasoning, and technological literacy through chapters, curriculum, and competition.

The sentence we keep

A nationwide student network where every chapter graduate can walk into a hospital, an insurance company, or a health-tech startup and already understand how those three worlds connect.

How to read it

Clinical, Financial, and Technology are the three rooms of the method. Case packets still use the internal name Care, Cost, Code. Chapters teach this across four tracks and twelve modules.

Name the gap

Name the condition, the patients or communities, and the gap in care. A team that skips this step writes a business plan for a problem it cannot describe.

Who is left out

Rural distance, uninsured families, and delayed diagnosis are clinical facts, not background color.

The money path

Insurance, hospital billing, grants, premiums, and unit economics sit here. If the money path fails, the clinical idea does not leave the page.

Chapter owner

A chapter Finance Lead owns this lens in meetings: Track 3 labs, budgets, and who actually pays.

What counts

Records, telehealth, devices, and simple tools count. The test is whether the tech changes the clinical or financial outcome, not whether it looks new.

Chapter owner

A chapter Technology Lead owns this lens: Track 2 modules and any digital project the chapter runs.
