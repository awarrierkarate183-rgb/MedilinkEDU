About

Who we are

Two high school students in Charlotte, North Carolina started MediLink because existing clubs split the work. One club trains clinical skill. Another trains business. A third treats technology as a side interest. Health systems do not work that way. We ask students to hold all three on one case.

01 / What we are

A student-founded network of high school chapters.

The work

Nonprofit chapter network. Clinical, money, and technology problems at once. One officer structure, one public syllabus, one competition calendar.

Not

Not a hospital foundation. Not a career-club franchise. Meetings, four tracks, twelve modules, five competitions.

Flagship

Nationals is the yearly flagship. The Apex is the biennial upgrade of that National round.

Mission

Train the next generation of healthcare problem-solvers by combining clinical understanding, financial reasoning, and technological literacy through chapters, curriculum, and competition.

02 / Vision

Walk into any of the three worlds already fluent.

A nationwide student network where every chapter graduate can walk into a hospital, an insurance company, or a health-tech startup and already understand how those three worlds connect.

03 / Clinical lens

What is the actual health problem, and who is affected?

Ask

Name the condition, the patients or communities, and the gap in care. Who is left out: rural distance, uninsured families, delayed diagnosis.

In Nationals

Care award: Best Clinical Insight. Skip this lens and the team writes a business plan for a problem it cannot describe.

04 / Financial lens

Who pays, what it costs, and whether it lasts.

Ask

Insurance, hospital billing, grants, premiums, unit economics. If the money path fails, the clinical idea stays on the page.

Owner

Chapter Finance Lead: Track 3 labs, budgets, who pays. Nationals Cost award: Best Financial Model.

05 / Technology lens

How data, software, or infrastructure changes access.

Ask

Records, telehealth, devices, simple tools. The test is outcome, not novelty.

Owner

Chapter Technology Lead: Track 2 and digital projects. Nationals Code award: Best Tech Innovation. Packets still say Care, Cost, Code.

06 / Aims

What we aim to achieve

The same six aims live on the Goals page. They describe the work, not results already in hand.

  • Train students to apply the three-lens framework to real health-system problems.
  • Grow a nationwide chapter network with a shared officer structure, curriculum, and starter kit.
  • Run four annual competitions plus a biennial Apex, with Nationals as the yearly flagship.
  • Connect members with clinical, finance, and health-tech partners for shadowing, internships, and research.
  • Give students titles, projects, and competition experience that transfer to college and career.
  • Give back through chapter service and outreach.
