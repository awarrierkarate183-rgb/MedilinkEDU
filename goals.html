Direction

Our Goals

A nationwide student network that produces people who already understand how hospitals, insurers, and health-tech companies connect. These six aims describe the work, not results already in hand. Open a file.

Train high school students to apply the three-lens framework to real health-system problems. About opens each lens. Packets still use the internal name Care, Cost, Code.

Grow a nationwide chapter network with a shared officer structure, curriculum, and starter kit. Founding, Established, and Flagship-Eligible are earned labels, not marketing badges.

Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium each run once a year. Only Nationals uses Regional to State to National. The Apex is not a sixth case.

This site is not a public job board and does not post legal requirements. Leadership sets up each relationship before students are placed.

Give students titles, projects, and competition experience that transfer to college and career. A title without work behind it does not count.

Give back to the community through chapter service and outreach. Homepage news waits for real work, not a hope statement.

Mission and vision

The sentences we keep

  • Mission. To train the next generation of healthcare problem-solvers by combining clinical understanding, financial reasoning, and technological literacy through chapters, curriculum, and competition.
  • Vision. A nationwide student network where every chapter graduate can walk into a hospital, an insurance company, or a health-tech startup and already understand how those three worlds connect.
