Direction

Our Goals

A nationwide student network that produces people who already understand how hospitals, insurers, and health-tech companies connect. These six aims describe the work, not results already in hand. About is the page that opens each lens. Mission and vision on this page must match About. If you change one, change both.

Six aims

What the work is for

Every module, case, and chapter project is supposed to run through the clinical, financial, and technology lenses. The aims below are the public list. They are not a scoreboard. Do not rewrite them as accomplishments until the board can point to the work.

01 / Train

Train high school students to apply the three-lens framework to real health-system problems. About opens each lens. Packets still use the internal name Care, Cost, Code. The public site names the lenses so a visitor does not need the packet to understand the method.

02 / Grow

Grow a nationwide chapter network with a shared officer structure, curriculum, and starter kit. Every chapter uses the same five offices so the framework has owners besides the two people who founded MediLink. Founding, Established, and Flagship-Eligible are earned labels, not marketing badges.

03 / Compete

Run four annual competitions plus a biennial Apex, with Nationals as the yearly flagship. Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium each run once a year. Only Nationals uses Regional to State to National. The Apex is not a sixth case.

04 / Connect

Connect members with clinical, finance, and health-tech partners for shadowing, internships, and research. Leadership sets up each relationship before students are placed. This site is not a public job board and does not post legal requirements.

05 / Transfer

Give students titles, projects, and competition experience that transfer to college and career. Officer roles, case work, and Nationals packets are meant to travel with the member. A title without work behind it does not count.

06 / Give back

Give back to the community through chapter service and outreach. The Outreach and Service Lead owns that work inside each chapter. Homepage news waits for real work, not a hope statement.

Mission and vision

The sentences we keep

  • Mission. To train the next generation of healthcare problem-solvers by combining clinical understanding, financial reasoning, and technological literacy through chapters, curriculum, and competition.
  • Vision. A nationwide student network where every chapter graduate can walk into a hospital, an insurance company, or a health-tech startup and already understand how those three worlds connect.
