Direction / Aims

Our Goals

A nationwide student network that produces people who already understand how hospitals, insurers, and health-tech companies connect.

01 / Train

Train on the three-lens framework

Real health-system problems, not split clubs.

Train high school students to apply the three-lens framework to real health-system problems. Every module, case, and chapter project runs through the clinical, financial, and technology lenses. About is the page that opens each lens.

02 / Grow

Grow a nationwide chapter network

Shared officer structure, curriculum, and starter kit.

Grow a nationwide chapter network with a shared officer structure, curriculum, and starter kit. Every chapter uses the same five offices so the framework has owners besides the two people who founded MediLink.

03 / Compete

Four annual events plus a biennial Apex

Nationals remains the yearly flagship.

Run four annual competitions plus a biennial Apex, with Nationals as the yearly flagship. Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium each run once a year. Only Nationals uses Regional to State to National.

04 / Connect

Partners in clinic, finance, and tech

Shadowing, internships, and research.

Connect members with clinical, finance, and health-tech partners for shadowing, internships, and research. Leadership sets up each relationship before students are placed.

05 / Transfer

Titles and projects that move on with the student

College and career, not only a club year.

Give students titles, projects, and competition experience that transfer to college and career. Officer roles, case work, and Nationals packets are meant to travel with the member.

06 / Give back

Chapter service and outreach

The network is not only inward-facing.

Give back to the community through chapter service and outreach. The Outreach and Service Lead owns that work inside each chapter.

Mission and vision

The sentences we keep

  • Mission. To train the next generation of healthcare problem-solvers by combining clinical understanding, financial reasoning, and technological literacy through chapters, curriculum, and competition.
  • Vision. A nationwide student network where every chapter graduate can walk into a hospital, an insurance company, or a health-tech startup and already understand how those three worlds connect.
