Direction

Our Goals

A nationwide student network that produces people who already understand how hospitals, insurers, and health-tech companies connect. These six aims describe the work, not results already in hand. Open a file.

Train high school students to apply the three-lens framework to real health-system problems. Every module, case, and project runs through clinic, money, and tech. About opens each lens at length. Packets still use the internal name Care, Cost, Code.

This aim is not a score we already hit. It is the work of Track 1 through Track 4 and of every Nationals case. A member who only studies one lens has not met this aim yet, even if that member is strong in that one room.

Where it lives

Public syllabus on Curriculum. Full lessons behind roster approval. Login is not live yet.

How we test it

Nationals teams of 3 to 4 must cover all three lenses. A Grand Prize still has to hold them together.

Grow a nationwide chapter network with a shared officer structure, curriculum, and starter kit. Founding, Established, and Flagship-Eligible are earned labels, not marketing badges. The first semester is not invented from scratch: five offices, starter agendas, Track 1 Module 1.1, and a sample local case challenge.

Listings currently cover North Carolina and Georgia. School names appear when the board records them. This site does not invent listings. Reactivation keeps a chapter's history and name when it has gone dark. That is different from starting over, and different from Chapter Support.

Same kit

Charter template. First 3 meeting agendas and decks. Track 1 Module 1.1. Sample local case challenge. Unique QR once the chapter is Founding.

Same five offices

President, VP of Operations, Finance / Treasury Lead, Technology Lead, Outreach and Service Lead. Volunteers back those offices. They do not replace them.

Run four annual competitions plus a biennial Apex, with Nationals as the yearly flagship. Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium each run once a year, with no regional or state qualifier. Only Nationals uses Regional to State to National. The Apex is not a sixth case.

You compete through a chapter. Dates come from state chapter boards. This site is not a calendar. Road to Apex standings come from a board-maintained ledger, not live scores.

One-time annuals

Pitch, brief, or poster. Individual, pairs, or teams of up to 3 depending on the event. Single placement on the ledger.

Flagship and summit

Nationals teams of 3 to 4 on the Track 4 capstone. Every other year that National round is enlarged. Same case, bigger year.

Connect members with clinical, finance, and health-tech partners for shadowing, internships, and research. This site is not a public job board and does not post legal requirements. Leadership sets up each relationship before students are placed. Shadowing still runs through partners and chapter advisors.

A useful partner sits in the same three-lens framework students use: Care-lens judges from hospitals and clinics, Cost-lens judges from insurers and healthcare finance, Code-lens judges from health-tech. A two-hour workshop mapped to a syllabus module is more useful than a generic pep talk.

Partner

Hospitals, insurers, health-tech firms, schools, and community groups. Open the Partner page for the four files.

Volunteer

Mentor, judge, or help a chapter run. Volunteers back the five student offices. They do not replace them.

Give students titles, projects, and competition experience that transfer to college and career. Officer roles, case work, and Nationals packets are the receipts. A title without work behind it does not count.

The five offices exist so the three-lens framework has owners besides the founders. Finance / Treasury Lead owns the financial lens inside the chapter. Technology Lead owns Track 2 and digital projects. Outreach and Service Lead owns service and the path for homepage news. President and VP of Operations keep that work on a calendar.

What transfers

A named office, a Track 4 case, a Nationals brief, a Policy Cup argument, a Research Symposium poster, or an Innovation Challenge pitch that a student can actually describe.

What does not

A logo on a resume and no file behind it. Fluency is not a license to practice and not a job offer from this website.

Give back to the community through chapter service and outreach. This aim is owned inside each chapter by the Outreach and Service Lead. Homepage news waits for real work, not a hope statement. This site does not invent school names or fake news.

Submit News is the public path for a chapter win, service project, or research highlight that actually happened. Leadership decides whether it runs on the homepage, on MediLink social, or at an event.

Chapter owner

Outreach and Service Lead: service, recruiting, and local partnerships. Volunteers and community liaisons back this office. They do not replace it.

How funds help

Individual and company gifts can go to service drives and local health-economics initiatives. A gift does not buy a student placement.

Mission and vision

The sentences we keep

  • Mission. To train the next generation of healthcare problem-solvers by combining clinical understanding, financial reasoning, and technological literacy through chapters, curriculum, and competition.
  • Vision. A nationwide student network where every chapter graduate can walk into a hospital, an insurance company, or a health-tech startup and already understand how those three worlds connect.
