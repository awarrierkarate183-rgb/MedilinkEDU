About MediLink

Our Goals

A nationwide student network that produces people who already understand how hospitals, insurers, and health-tech companies connect.

What We Aim to Achieve

Six Core Goals

  • Train high school students to apply Care, Cost, and Code to real health-system problems.
  • Grow a nationwide chapter network with a shared officer structure, curriculum, and starter kit.
  • Run four national competitions on a Regional → State → National ladder, with Nationals as the flagship.
  • Connect members with clinical, finance, and health-tech partners for shadowing, internships, and research.
  • Give students titles, projects, and competition experience that transfer to college and career.
  • Give back to the community through chapter service and outreach.

What We Stand For

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

MediLink trains students to solve healthcare problems the way real health systems are solved — by combining clinical understanding, financial reasoning, and technology, through chapters, competitions, and hands-on projects.

Our Vision

A nationwide student network where every chapter produces students who can walk into a hospital, a health insurance company, or a health-tech startup and already understand how those three worlds connect.
