Four National Competitions

MediLink competitions help high school students apply research and strategy to real health and economic problems. Every competition feeds a shared national brand and runs on the same Regional → State → National ladder.

Flagship Competition

MediLink Nationals

The signature event. Teams of 3–4 — covering Care, Cost, and Code — tackle a real health-system case in three phases: remote prelims, regional presentations, and a national final plus awards gala. This is the event where sponsors receive top billing.

Explore Nationals

MediLink Innovation Challenge

A pitch competition for a health-tech product or digital-health concept — an app, device concept, or platform idea. Judged on feasibility, impact, and a basic business case. The entry point for tech-minded members.

View Challenge

MediLink Policy Cup

A policy brief and advocacy competition where students argue for a real fix to a health-economics problem — insurance access, rural care deserts, drug pricing, and more. Debate and presentation format.

View Policy Cup

MediLink Research Symposium

A poster-and-presentation competition for members already doing independent research on a health-economics or health-tech topic. Lower barrier to entry — a strong path for newer members.

View Symposium

How Advancement Works

Regional → State → National

Every competition uses the same three-tier ladder. What happens at each level is set by the state chapter board each year.

Level 1

Regional

The first competitive level, run at or near individual chapters and open to all active members.

Level 2

State

Top performers from regional events advance. Coordinated by the state chapter board.

Level 3

National

Top state qualifiers compete nationally — for Nationals, this includes the awards gala.
