Compete

Five competitions. Two structures.

Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium each run once a year, with no regional or state qualifier. Nationals is the only event that climbs Regional to State to National. Every other year, that National round becomes the Apex. The Apex is not a sixth case. Dates come from state chapter boards through chapter leaders. This page is the structure, not a calendar. You compete through a chapter, not as a visitor who clicked a public sign-up.

Yearly flagship

MediLink Nationals

Nationals is the yearly flagship. Teams of 3 to 4 work the same Track 4 capstone case. That link is deliberate. Curriculum and competition stay joined because the case comes from the syllabus the chapters already ran. Packets still use the internal name Care, Cost, Code for the three lenses. Public pages name those lenses clinical, financial, and technology unless they are quoting the packet.

A Nationals year includes a written brief and video pitch, live regionals, a national final, and an awards evening. State boards set dates. The intended home for the National round is Charlotte. In an Apex year the National round is enlarged. It is still Nationals. It is not a replacement event with a new case.

Who

Grades 9-12 in a Flagship-Eligible chapter, or a chapter on that path. Teams must cover clinical, financial, and technology. A three-person team still has to hold all three lenses. Do not enter as a single specialty club.

Awards

Best Clinical Insight, Best Financial Model, Best Tech Innovation, and a Grand Prize across the three-lens framework. Sponsors can name a track or award. Naming does not change the method the judges use.

The Nationals ladder

Regional. State. National.

Only Nationals uses this climb. Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium do not have regional or state qualifiers. If a student asks how to "qualify" for Policy Cup, the answer is: enter that annual event through your chapter. There is no sub-ladder.

Nationals has three phases inside the year and three levels on the ladder. Phases describe the work product. Levels describe where the team stands. Phase 1 is Case Release: a remote brief plus a short video pitch. Phase 2 is Regional Rounds: a live presentation to judges from hospitals, finance firms, and health-tech. Phase 3 is National Finals: a live final and awards evening.

Level 1 / Regional

First Nationals round, run at or near chapters. Top regional teams advance. This is the round that turns a Founding or Established chapter toward Flagship-Eligible once the chapter has actually participated.

Level 2 / State

The state chapter board coordinates this round. Dates are not posted as a public calendar on this site. Ask your chapter leader. Top state teams advance to National.

Level 3 / National

State qualifiers compete nationally. Live final and awards evening. In an Apex year, this level is the summit. The case is still the Nationals case, not a new sixth prompt.

Points

Nationals points add up as a team advances. That is why Nationals is worth more on the Road to Apex than a one-time event. There is no extra multiplier. The ledger is on this page under Road to Apex.

One annual event

Innovation Challenge

Innovation Challenge is a pitch for an app, device concept, or platform idea that closes a gap in care, access, or operations. Feasibility, impact, and a basic business case matter. A finished product is not required. No qualifier rounds. There is no regional Innovation Challenge and no state Innovation Challenge.

Track 4 includes pitch prep for this event. The Technology Lead often coaches the digital side, but a strong pitch still has to name the clinical gap and who pays. This event is not a substitute for the technology lens on a Nationals case. It is its own annual competition with its own Apex points on the one-time schedule.

Who

Grades 9-12, active chapter. Individual or teams of up to 3. You compete through the chapter, not as an unaffiliated visitor.

Expect

One annual deck and live pitch. Apex points on the one-time schedule: participation, top 10, top 3, or a win. The board records a single placement, not a ladder.

One annual event

Policy Cup

Policy Cup is a brief and a debate. Students research a live issue, write a brief, and argue a concrete reform. It is debate and presentation, not an exam. Topics come from the state chapter board. This site does not invent this year's topic. Ask your chapter leader when the board releases it.

No qualifier rounds. Individual or pairs. Track 4 includes brief-writing prep. A strong brief still has to hold clinic, money, and systems, even though this is not Nationals. Apex points sit on the one-time schedule.

Who

Grades 9-12, active chapter. Individual or pairs. Topics from the state chapter board, not from this website.

Expect

One annual brief plus live advocacy. Apex points on the one-time schedule. There is no Policy Cup regionals ladder.

One annual event

Research Symposium

Research Symposium is independent research presented as a poster and a short talk. It is the lowest barrier of the five. A student who is not ready for a four-person Nationals team can still enter here. The subject area is health economics or health tech. A poster that cannot name a question, a method, and a finding is not finished.

No qualifier rounds. Track 4 includes poster prep. Apex points sit on the one-time schedule. Mentors can help, and the volunteer page is the path for an adult who wants to guide a project. Leadership still sets up any partner relationship before students are placed in a research setting.

Who

Grades 9-12, active chapter, working in health economics or health tech research. Individual work is expected more often here than on Nationals teams.

Expect

One annual poster plus oral. Apex points on the one-time schedule. Lowest barrier of the five, not a lesser event. Champions can still be invited to the Apex.

Biennial culmination

The MediLink Apex

The Apex is not a sixth case. Nationals stays the yearly flagship. Every two years that National round is enlarged into a summit. Top performers from Nationals plus champions from Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium are invited. A new two-year cycle starts at zero after each Apex.

The summit includes a championship round across all four disciplines, a sponsor networking fair, keynotes from medicine, finance, and health-tech, and an awards gala. Impact Partner and Visionary Sponsor can attend the fair in an Apex year. Community Ally and Health Advocate do not receive that in-person perk unless they move up a named corporate tier.

Who is invited

Top performers from Nationals plus champions from the three one-time annual events. Qualification also uses the Road to Apex ledger: ranking plus a regional guarantee. The board updates the ledger after each event. These numbers are not live scores.

What it includes

Championship round across all four disciplines. Sponsor networking fair. Keynotes from medicine, finance, and health-tech. Awards gala. Still the Nationals case in an enlarged year, not a new prompt.
