Compete

Five competitions. Two structures.

Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium each run once a year, with no regional or state qualifier. Nationals is the only event that climbs Regional to State to National. Every other year, that National round becomes the Apex. Dates come from state chapter boards through chapter leaders. This page is the structure, not a calendar.

Yearly flagship

MediLink Nationals

Teams of 3 to 4. Same Track 4 capstone case for every team.

Who

Grades 9-12 in a Flagship-Eligible chapter, or a chapter on that path. Teams must cover clinical, financial, and technology.

Year

Written brief and video pitch, live regionals, national final and awards gala. State boards set dates. Intended home: Charlotte.

Awards

Best Clinical Insight, Best Financial Model, Best Tech Innovation, Grand Prize across the three-lens framework. Sponsors can name a track or award.

Packets

Internal name Care, Cost, Code. Curriculum and competition stay linked because the case comes from Track 4.

The Nationals ladder

Regional. State. National.

Only Nationals uses this climb. Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium do not have regional or state qualifiers.

Phase 1

Case Release. Remote brief plus a short video pitch.

Phase 2

Regional Rounds. Live presentation to judges from hospitals, finance firms, and health-tech.

Phase 3

National Finals. Live final and awards evening.

Ladder

Only Nationals: Regional, then State, then National. The other three events have no sub-levels.

Level 1

Regional

First Nationals round, run at or near chapters.

Level 2

State

Top regional teams advance. The state board coordinates this round.

Level 3

National

State qualifiers compete nationally. Live final and awards evening. In an Apex year, this level is the summit.

One annual event

Innovation Challenge

A pitch for an app, device concept, or platform idea. No qualifier rounds.

What

Pitch a health-tech idea for a gap in care, access, or operations. Feasibility, impact, basic business case. Finished product not required.

Who / expect

Grades 9-12, active chapter. Individual or teams of up to 3. One annual deck and live pitch. Apex points on the one-time schedule.

One annual event

Policy Cup

A brief and debate. Topics come from the state chapter board.

What

Research a live issue, write a brief, argue a concrete reform. Debate and presentation, not an exam. Topics from the state chapter board.

Who / expect

Grades 9-12, active chapter. Individual or pairs. One annual brief plus live advocacy. Apex points on the one-time schedule.

One annual event

Research Symposium

A poster and talk. Lowest barrier of the five.

What

Independent research as a poster and short talk. Lowest barrier of the five.

Who / expect

Grades 9-12, active chapter, health economics or health tech research. One annual poster plus oral. Apex points on the one-time schedule.

Biennial culmination

The MediLink Apex

Not a sixth case. Nationals stays the yearly flagship. Every two years that National round is enlarged.

Who is invited

Top performers from Nationals plus champions from Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium.

What it includes

Championship round across all four disciplines. Sponsor networking fair. Keynotes from medicine, finance, and health-tech. Awards gala.
