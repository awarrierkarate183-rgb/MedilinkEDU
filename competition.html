Five competitions. Two structures.

Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium each run once a year, with no regional or state qualifier. Nationals is the only event that climbs Regional to State to National. Every other year, that National round becomes the Apex, a summit built from top performers across the other four. Open a tile for format, who can enter, and what a year actually looks like.

What it is

The yearly flagship. Same case for every team. Curriculum and competition stay connected because the case comes from Track 4.

Who can participate

Grades 9-12 in a Flagship-Eligible chapter, or a chapter on that path. Teams of 3 to 4 must cover all three lenses.

What to expect

Written brief and video pitch, then live regional rounds, then a national final and awards gala. State boards set dates.

Phase 1. Case Release

Remote brief plus a short video pitch. Packets use the internal name Care, Cost, Code.

Phase 2. Regional Rounds

Top teams present live to judges from hospitals, finance firms, and health-tech companies.

Phase 3. National Finals

Live final and awards evening. The intended home is Charlotte.

Awards

Best Clinical Insight, Best Financial Model, Best Tech Innovation, and a Grand Prize for strength across the three-lens framework. Sponsors can name a track or award.

Level 1. Regional

First Nationals round, run at or near chapters. Only Nationals uses this ladder.

Level 2. State

Top regional teams advance. The state board coordinates this round.

Level 3. National

State qualifiers compete nationally. In an Apex year, this level is the summit.

What it is

Students pitch a health-tech idea that addresses a gap in care, access, or operations. Judges score feasibility, impact, and a basic business case. A finished product is not required.

Who can participate

Grades 9-12 in an active MediLink chapter. Individual or teams of up to 3.

What to expect

One annual pitch deck and live presentation. Top finishers earn Apex points on the one-time schedule.
