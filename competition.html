Five competitions. Two structures.

Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium each run once a year, with no regional or state qualifier. Nationals is the only event that climbs Regional to State to National. Every other year that National round becomes the Apex. The Apex is not a sixth case. This page is the structure, not a calendar. Open a file.

Grades 9-12 in a Flagship-Eligible chapter, or a chapter on that path. Teams must cover clinical, financial, and technology. A year includes a written brief and video pitch, live regionals, a national final, and an awards evening. State boards set dates. Intended home: Charlotte.

Awards

Best Clinical Insight, Best Financial Model, Best Tech Innovation, Grand Prize. Sponsors can name a track or award. Naming does not change the method judges use.

Packets

Phase 1: Case Release. Remote brief plus a short video pitch. Phase 2: Regional Rounds. Live presentation to judges from hospitals, finance firms, and health-tech. Phase 3: National Finals. Live final and awards evening.

Level 1 / Regional

First Nationals round, run at or near chapters. Participating here is part of the path to Flagship-Eligible.

Level 2 / State

The state chapter board coordinates this round. Ask your chapter leader for dates.

Level 3 / National

State qualifiers compete nationally. In an Apex year, this level is the summit. Same case, enlarged year.

Points

Nationals points add as a team advances. That is why Nationals is worth more on the Road to Apex. No extra multiplier.

Independent research as a poster and a short talk. Health economics or health tech. A poster that cannot name a question, a method, and a finding is not finished. Grades 9-12, active chapter. One annual poster plus oral. Champions can still be invited to the Apex.

Every two years the National round is enlarged. Top performers from Nationals plus champions from Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium are invited. Championship round across all four disciplines. Sponsor networking fair. Keynotes from medicine, finance, and health-tech. Awards gala.

Impact Partner and Visionary Sponsor can attend the fair in an Apex year. A new two-year cycle starts at zero after each Apex. Qualification also uses the Road to Apex ledger.
