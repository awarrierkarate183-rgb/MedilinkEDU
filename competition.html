Competitions & Events · Grades 9–12

Four Competitions. One Event Ladder.

MediLink competitions help high school students apply Care, Cost, and Code to real health and economic problems. Every competition feeds a shared national brand and runs Regional → State → National.

Flagship Competition

MediLink Nationals

The signature event. Teams of 3–4 — covering Care, Cost, and Code — tackle a real health-system case in three phases: remote prelims, regional presentations, and a national final plus awards gala. This is the event where sponsors receive top billing.

Explore Nationals

MediLink Innovation Challenge

A pitch competition for a health-tech product or digital-health concept — an app, device concept, or platform idea. Judged on feasibility, impact, and a basic business case. The entry point for tech-minded members.

MediLink Policy Cup

A policy brief and advocacy competition where students argue for a real fix to a health-economics problem — insurance access, rural care deserts, drug pricing, and more. Debate and presentation format.

View Policy Cup

MediLink Research Symposium

A poster-and-presentation competition for members already doing independent research on a health-economics or health-tech topic. Lower barrier to entry — a strong path for newer members.

Conferences & Events

Regional → State → National

Every competition uses the same three-tier ladder. This describes the structure — not a specific year’s calendar.

The specific dates, formats, and requirements for each level are set each year by the state chapter board and communicated to members through their individual chapter leaders. This page describes the structure — check with your chapter leader for what's happening this year.

Level 1

Regional Events

The first competitive level, run at or near individual chapters, open to all active members. This is where most students compete for the first time.

Level 2

State Events

Top performers from regional events advance. State events are run at the state level and coordinated by the state chapter board.

Level 3

National Events

The top state qualifiers compete nationally. For MediLink Nationals, this culminates in the awards gala — the signature gathering of the year.
