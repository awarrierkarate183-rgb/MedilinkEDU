Five competitions. Two structures.

Innovation Challenge, Policy Cup, and Research Symposium each run once a year. Nationals is the only event that climbs Regional to State to National. Every other year, that National round becomes the Apex.

MediLink Nationals

The yearly flagship. Teams of 3 to 4 take a health-system case drawn from that year’s Track 4 capstone and climb Regional to State to National, ending in a final and awards gala.

Explore Nationals

  • Policy Cup

  • Research Symposium

Nationals and the Apex

Nationals happens every year. The Apex happens every two years by enlarging that cycle’s National round. Same qualification path. A bigger gathering, including a sponsor networking fair.

How the Apex works

The Nationals ladder

Only MediLink Nationals uses Regional to State to National. The other three annual events do not have sub-levels.

Dates and materials come from the state chapter board each year, through chapter leaders. This page is the structure, not a calendar.

Level 1

Regional Events

First Nationals round, run at or near chapters, open to active members.

Level 2

State Events

Top regional Nationals teams advance. The state chapter board coordinates this round.

Level 3

National Events

State Nationals qualifiers compete nationally. In an Apex year, this level is the multi-day summit.
