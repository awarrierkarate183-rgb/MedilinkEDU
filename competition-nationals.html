MediLink Nationals

The yearly flagship. Teams of 3 to 4 take a health-system case drawn from that year's Track 4 capstone and climb Regional to State to National, ending in a final and awards gala. This is the only competition with that ladder.

Three phases

Every team gets the same case. Curriculum and competition stay connected because the case comes from that year's Track 4 capstone. State boards set dates and materials.

Phase 1

Case Release (Prelims)

Done remotely. Teams submit a written brief plus a short video pitch. A team of 3 to 4 must cover the three-lens framework. Packets still use the internal name Care, Cost, Code.

Phase 2

Regional Rounds

Top teams from each region present live to judge panels from partner hospitals, finance firms, and health-tech companies. All three lenses count equally.

Phase 3

National Finals and Gala

Top teams compete live, then an awards evening. The intended home for finals is Charlotte. This is the gathering built for press, sponsors, and chapter recruiting.

Awards

Sponsors can put their name on a track or award. The Grand Prize requires strength across the three-lens framework.

🩺

Best Clinical Insight

The Care award. The team that best names the health problem and who is affected.

💹

Best Financial Model

The Cost award. The team that best answers who pays, what it costs, and whether it is sustainable.

💻

Best Tech Innovation

The Code award. The team that best uses data, software, devices, or infrastructure to change access or delivery.

🏆

Grand Prize

Requires excelling at all three lenses. This is the headline award of the gala.

Who Can Participate

Open to high school students in grades 9-12 who are active members of a Flagship-Eligible MediLink chapter, or a chapter on the path to that status. Teams have 3 to 4 students and must cover all three lenses.

How to Register

You enter through a MediLink chapter at your high school. Chapter leaders receive this year's case, deadlines, and round details from the state chapter board.

Practice first

Chapters can run a local case challenge using the same Care, Cost, Code packet format. That local run is practice for Nationals. See the Chapter Starter Kit.

The full Case + Pitch + Gala format is the destination structure. Local and regional pilots come first so chapters can learn the format before a large finals weekend. Ask your chapter leader what is running this year. This page describes the structure, not a published calendar.
