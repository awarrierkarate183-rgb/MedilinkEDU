MediLink Research Symposium

A poster-and-presentation competition for members doing independent research on a health-economics or health-tech topic. Lowest barrier to entry of the five — a strong on-ramp for newer or younger members who aren’t ready for a full case competition yet.

What It Is

Students share original or independent research through a poster and a short presentation. Lowest barrier to entry of the five competitions — a strong on-ramp for newer members.

Who Can Participate

Open to high school students in grades 9–12 who are active members of a MediLink chapter and are working on (or have completed) independent research in health economics or health tech.

What to Expect

Poster display plus an oral presentation, advancing Regional → State → National. Submission rules and poster specs are set each year by the state chapter board.

Compete Through Your Chapter

Participation is through a MediLink chapter at your high school. Ask your chapter leader for this year’s Symposium timeline.
