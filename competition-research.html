MediLink Research Symposium

One annual poster and talk. Built for members who already have a research question in health economics or health tech. The lowest barrier of the five events. There is no regional or state qualifier.

What It Is

Students share original or independent research through a poster and a short presentation. Newer members who are not ready for a full case competition often start here.

Who Can Participate

Open to high school students in grades 9-12 who are active members of a MediLink chapter and are working on, or have finished, independent research in health economics or health tech.

What to Expect

A single annual poster display plus an oral presentation. The state chapter board sets submission rules and poster specs each year. Top finishers earn Apex points on the one-time schedule.

Compete through your chapter

You enter through a MediLink chapter at your high school. Ask your chapter leader for this year's Symposium timeline.
