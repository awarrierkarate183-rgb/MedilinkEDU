Status

Status and standings

This page only shows a recorded status and this cycle's Apex points. Founding, Established, and Flagship-Eligible are defined on the Start a Chapter page. Founding means chartered, officers in place, meetings running. Established means at least one internal event or case challenge and 10 or more active members. Flagship-Eligible means the chapter has participated in a Nationals regional round and may send teams to Nationals.

Full rankings live on the Road to Apex. Points come from a board-maintained ledger. These numbers are not live scores. Nationals is the only ladder. The other three annual events use the one-time schedule.
