Growing a Student Network

MediLink chapters bring students together to learn the three-lens framework, lead, and serve in their own communities. Chapters grow from Founding to Established to Flagship-Eligible as they run meetings, host events, and compete.

Founding, Established, Flagship-Eligible

This is how we talk about chapter progress instead of empty placeholder listings.

Status 1

Founding Chapter

Chartered, has officers, and is running meetings.

Status 2

Established Chapter

Has run at least one internal event or case challenge, and has 10+ active members.

Status 3

Flagship-Eligible Chapter

Has participated in a regional competition and may send teams to Nationals.
